Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Summary

Better PDF Export processes your document entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, store or transmit your documents, your pictures, or any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies.

What the add-in does with your document

When you click Export PDF with original pictures, the add-in asks Word for two things: the PDF that Word's own converter produces, and a copy of the document package (the .docx file). Both stay in the memory of Word's task pane. The add-in replaces the compressed pictures in the PDF with the original ones from the document package and writes the result to the location you choose. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Network access

The add-in's program files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS and icons) are static files served from GitHub Pages at rakantor.github.io, and the Office JavaScript library is loaded from Microsoft's content delivery network (appsforoffice.microsoft.com) as required for all Office add-ins. Loading these files reveals your IP address to those providers in the same way visiting any website does; see GitHub's privacy statement and Microsoft's privacy statement. Beyond loading these files, the add-in makes no network requests.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate a server that receives data from the add-in.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected.
